Methods, sources, and interpretation limits

OPM EHRI Status reports active employees at month end. Coverage excludes some federal populations, and submissions can be delayed or revised. The dashboard uses aggregate counts from the six local employment files. Its occupational-series selection is an analyst-defined proxy; the figures do not establish that AI adoption caused staffing changes.
