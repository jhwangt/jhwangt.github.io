Research use and limits

OPM EHRI Status reports active employees at the end of each month. Coverage excludes some federal populations, and agency submissions may be revised or delayed. Compare only like agency groupings, and check OPM data quality before drawing conclusions. This dashboard contains aggregate counts only.

Potential thesis questions: Which agencies gained or lost capacity? Did selected technical series change differently from overall employment? Are changes concentrated in a few agencies? The separate AI inventory dashboard can support exploratory cross-sectional comparisons; its 2024 use-case counts are not a time series.
